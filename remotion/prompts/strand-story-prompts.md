# The Strand Invitational — story prompts for image and video generation

34 scenes, one per story, drawn from the group emails (2018–2026) and the site history.

## How to use these

1. **Paste the style line** below in front of any image prompt so every scene matches.
2. **Faces:** a text prompt cannot make someone look like the real person. For each scene, attach a reference photo of the player named in *Cast* (the headshots in `remotion/public/players/` work) and keep the words "the man in the reference photo" in the prompt.
3. **Make the still first, then animate it** with the *Motion* line in an image-to-video tool. 4–6 seconds each is plenty.
4. *Caption* is the on-screen text for the edit, not part of the prompt. Keep text out of the generated image; add it in the edit.

**Style line (prepend to every image prompt):**

> Cinematic sports-documentary still, shot on 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field, warm golden-hour light, subtle film grain, deadpan comedic tone played completely straight, photorealistic, 16:9, no text, no logos.

## 2018 — Palm Springs (the first one)

### 1. The hole-in-one that got confiscated
*Source: Nick's ace, and the ball later being taken — "give that back."*
*Cast: Nick*
- **Image:** A golfer, the man in the reference photo, on a palm-lined desert par 3, frozen mid-celebration with both arms up, mouth open in disbelief. Behind him, three friends are not celebrating — one is calmly walking off toward the cart with the golf ball held up between two fingers like evidence.
- **Motion:** He turns from joy to confusion as he notices the friend walking away with the ball; slow push-in on his face.
- **Caption:** "Give that back."

### 2. The self-appointed Chairman
*Source: Drew signing emails as "2018 Victor / Chairman."*
*Cast: Drew*
- **Image:** The man in the reference photo seated behind an enormous mahogany executive desk placed absurdly in the middle of a desert fairway, wearing a golf polo and a too-big blazer, hands steepled, a tiny plastic trophy on the desk, palm trees and mountains behind him.
- **Motion:** Slow dolly-in; he gives one small, satisfied nod. A sprinkler turns on behind him.
- **Caption:** "2018 Victor. Chairman. Self-appointed."

### 3. 49 to 38
*Source: Drew & Kev beat Justin & Rhett 49–38.*
*Cast: Drew, Kev (winners); Justin, Rhett (not)*
- **Image:** Split composition on an 18th green at sunset: on the left two golfers pose back-to-back like an action-movie poster; on the right two golfers sit slumped on a golf cart bench staring at a scorecard.
- **Motion:** Winners hold the pose a beat too long; on the right, one loser slowly crumples the scorecard.
- **Caption:** "Year one. It was not close."

## 2019 — Indio (Serenity of the Desert)

### 4. "We'll mark you down as a 12"
*Source: Rhett — "haven't broken 100 in years… 130 very possible." Fred — "we'll mark you down as a 12."*
*Cast: Fred, Rhett*
- **Image:** A stern tournament official, the man in the reference photo, at a folding registration table in a desert parking lot, writing in a ledger without looking up. Across from him a golfer pleads with open palms, golf bag sliding off his shoulder.
- **Motion:** The official stamps the page with finality; the golfer's shoulders drop.
- **Caption:** "Claimed handicap: 30. Official handicap: 12."

### 5. The sandbagger
*Source: Fred's recap — "Congratulations to the Putt Pirates and Rhett's sandbagging!" Rhett won low net both days.*
*Cast: Rhett*
- **Image:** The man in the reference photo walking off a green in slow-motion hero framing, carrying two actual burlap sandbags over his shoulders like trophies, cash sticking out of his back pocket, four friends glaring from a cart in the background.
- **Motion:** Slow-motion strut toward camera; one bill flutters out of his pocket.
- **Caption:** "Low net. Both days. Curious."

### 6. One arm
*Source: Justin — "If anyone is a 30 in this group I hope they only have one arm."*
*Cast: Justin*
- **Image:** The man in the reference photo on a driving range, squinting with deep suspicion at a row of golfers down the line, arms crossed, clipboard tucked under one arm, like a detective at a lineup.
- **Motion:** Slow pan down the line of golfers, each one avoiding eye contact; cut back to his narrowed eyes.
- **Caption:** "Handicap verification, 2019."

### 7. Just here for the burritos
*Source: Wix's first Strand — "just going for breakfast burritos." Jordan's burritos were "phenomenal."*
*Cast: Wix, Jordan*
- **Image:** A desert rental-house kitchen at sunrise. One man at the stove assembling breakfast burritos with total focus, a tall stack of foil-wrapped burritos gleaming like gold bars. In the doorway, the man in the reference photo in full golf outfit, holding a plate out, golf clubs forgotten on the floor behind him.
- **Motion:** Rack focus from the burrito stack to his hopeful face; steam rises.
- **Caption:** "Reason for attending: burritos."

### 8. Worst to almost-first
*Source: Fred — "Wix… You went from high net to second low net in just one day… while narrowly escaping a terminal condition (or whatever that guy said)."*
*Cast: Wix*
- **Image:** Two-panel still. Left: the man in the reference photo face-down in a bunker at midday, hat a few feet away. Right: the same man the next day at golden hour holding a modest second-place envelope, looking as surprised as anyone.
- **Motion:** Left panel: he lifts his head and spits sand. Wipe to right panel: he opens the envelope and looks to camera.
- **Caption:** "Day 1: last. Day 2: second. Doctors baffled."

### 9. Through a blizzard
*Source: Matty O "driving thru a blizzard and making a bunch of pars even though he hasn't played in a year."*
*Cast: Matty O*
- **Image:** A sedan crawling over a mountain pass in a heavy snowstorm at night, golf clubs strapped to the roof and crusted with ice, the driver — the man in the reference photo — leaning forward gripping the wheel, wearing a golf visor.
- **Motion:** Wipers beat, snow streaks past; match cut to the same man in bright desert sun calmly tapping in a par putt.
- **Caption:** "Hadn't played in a year. Drove through a blizzard. Made pars."

### 10. Removed from the thread
*Source: Drew being removed from the email chain by Schroe.*
*Cast: Drew, Schroe*
- **Image:** A group of golfers laughing around a patio fire pit at dusk, all looking at their phones. Outside the sliding glass door, alone in the dark, one man presses his hand to the glass, phone in the other hand showing nothing.
- **Motion:** Slow push through the laughing group to the man at the glass; he taps the glass once.
- **Caption:** "Drew has left the conversation. (Involuntarily.)"

### 11. Away from Honey
*Source: Fred thanking Schroe "for being away from his puppy Honey."*
*Cast: Schroe*
- **Image:** The man in the reference photo on a tee box, staring wistfully at a phone lock-screen photo of a small puppy, his playing partners waiting behind him with arms spread in impatience.
